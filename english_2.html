Teaching Tip

Before the exercises, have participants create 2–3 of their own example sentences using at least five words from the list. This activates the vocabulary before moving on to the tasks.

Further Discussion Activities

For follow-up practice – speaking and discussing in pairs or small groups.

👥
A · Partner Work

Partner Interview

Interview each other using the questions from Exercise 4. Take notes on your partner's answers.

  • What type of house does your partner live in?
  • What does he/she particularly like about his/her home?
  • Present your partner's answers to the group using reported speech: "He said he lives in…"
💬
B · Small Group (3–4)

Group Discussion: "My Dream House"

Discuss the following questions in small groups:

  • If you could live anywhere in the world, where would your dream house be?
  • Would your dream house be in the city, in the countryside, or by the sea?
  • What are the three most important rooms or features for you? Why?
  • Would you rather have a big house far from the centre, or a small flat in the centre?
⇌
C · Partner Work

Compare and Evaluate (Comparatives)

Compare your current home with your dream house and practise comparative forms:

  • My dream house would be bigger / smaller than my house now.
  • My dream house would be more modern / more beautiful / quieter than my house now.

Then share your ideas with another pair and report what you learned about your partner.

🏠
D · Partner Work · Role Play

House Viewing

Work in pairs. One person is the estate agent, the other is the house hunter.

  • The estate agent describes a house (number of rooms, location, special features).
  • The house hunter asks questions, e.g. "Is there a garden?", "How many bedrooms are there?"
  • At the end, the house hunter decides whether they would like to rent or buy the house – and explains why.
✎
E · Partner Work · Writing

Short Discussion Text

Write a short paragraph together (5–6 sentences) with the title "The Perfect Home". Then read your text to another pair and compare your ideas.
