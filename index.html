Teaching Tip

Before starting the speaking exercises, have participants brainstorm three jobs they would love to do and three they would hate. Ask them to use the new vocabulary (e.g., salary, working hours, remote work) to explain their choices.

Further Discussion Activities

For follow-up practice – speaking and discussing in pairs or small groups.

👥
A · Partner Work · Role Play

The Job Interview

Work in pairs. One person is the HR manager (employer), the other is the applicant.

  • The HR manager asks 3-4 questions (e.g. "Why do you want this job?", "What are your strengths?").
  • The applicant answers and asks at least two questions about the job (e.g. salary, working hours).
  • Swap roles after 5 minutes!
💬
B · Small Group (3–4)

Group Discussion: "The Perfect Job"

Discuss the following questions in your group:

  • What is more important to you: a high salary or loving what you do? Why?
  • Do you prefer working in an office with colleagues or remote work?
  • What makes a good boss or employer?
  • At what age do you think people should retire?
↔
C · Partner Work

Compare Professions (Comparatives)

Compare different jobs using comparative adjectives (e.g., stressful, dangerous, well-paid, boring):

  • A doctor earns more than a teacher, but a teacher's job can be just as stressful.
  • Working in an office is safer than working on a construction site.

Think of at least 5 comparisons and present your favorite one to the class.

💡
D · Partner Work · Problem Solving

Career Advice

Student A is unhappy with their current job (e.g., the commute is too long, the boss is mean, the salary is low). Student B gives advice.

  • Student A explains the problem using the new vocabulary.
  • Student B uses phrases like "Why don't you...", "You should...", or "Have you thought about..." to help.
✎
E · Individual or Partner Work · Writing

My Dream Job Profile

Write a short paragraph (5–6 sentences) describing your absolute dream job. Include details about:

  • What the daily tasks would be.
  • Your preferred working hours and location.
  • Why this job would make you happy.
